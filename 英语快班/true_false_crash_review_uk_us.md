# 英美常识 True/False 高效复习：先知识点，后做题

适用题型：10 道 True or False。  
复习原则：判断题不是靠猜，而是靠“概念边界 + 关键词 + 易错反转”。  
重点顺序：**英美政府 > 美国历史 > 英美教育 > 地理/文学/经济补充**。

## 0. 判断题核心方法

### 先看知识点，再看题

判断题常把正确知识点改错一处，例如：

- 把 England 写成 the UK。
- 把 British monarch 写成 real ruler。
- 把 US President 写成 directly elected by popular vote。
- 把 American education 写成 controlled by the federal government。
- 把 American Revolution 的 cause 写成 slavery，而不是 taxation/rights。

### 看到这些词要小心

- **only** 只要
- **all** 所有
- **always** 总是
- **never** 从不
- **directly** 直接
- **completely** 完全
- **the same as** 等同于

这些词容易把本来正确的句子变成 False。

# Part 1 重点知识点：中英对照

## 1. 英国政府 British Government

### 1.1 Constitutional Monarchy 宪政君主制

核心句：

> The British monarch reigns but does not rule.  
> 英国君主“统而不治”。

必背知识：

- **monarch 君主**：King or Queen，是 **Head of State 国家元首**。
- **Prime Minister 首相**：Head of Government 政府首脑，真正领导政府。
- **constitutional monarchy 宪政君主制**：君主权力受宪法原则、议会和惯例限制。
- 英国君主仍有 ceremonial and formal roles 礼仪性和形式性职责：
  - formally appoint the Prime Minister 正式任命首相。
  - give Royal Assent to bills 对法案给予御准。
  - open Parliament 主持议会开幕。
  - meet the Prime Minister regularly 定期会见首相。
  - represent the nation ceremonially 在礼仪场合代表国家。

判断题易错：

- The British monarch is the real ruler of Britain. **False**
- The monarch is the Head of State. **True**
- The Prime Minister is the Head of Government. **True**
- The monarch reigns but does not rule. **True**

### 1.2 Parliament 议会

核心句：

> Parliament is the supreme law-making authority in Britain.  
> 英国议会是最高立法机关。

必背知识：

- **Parliament 议会** 严格包括三部分：
  - the monarch 君主
  - the House of Lords 上议院
  - the House of Commons 下议院
- **House of Commons 下议院**：
  - MPs are elected by voters. 议员由选民选出。
  - about 650 MPs. 大约 650 名议员。
  - real centre of political power. 真正政治权力中心。
  - controls finance, legislation and government accountability. 控制财政、立法和政府问责。
- **House of Lords 上议院**：
  - unelected upper chamber. 非选举产生的上院。
  - revises and delays legislation. 修改和延缓法案。
  - weaker than the House of Commons. 权力弱于下议院。
- **parliamentary sovereignty 议会主权**：
  - Parliament can make, change or repeal any law. 议会可以制定、修改或废除任何法律。
  - No other body can override an Act of Parliament in the traditional doctrine. 传统理论上没有其他机构能推翻议会法案。

判断题易错：

- Parliament means only the House of Commons and the House of Lords. **False**
- The House of Commons is elected. **True**
- The House of Lords is stronger than the House of Commons. **False**
- Parliamentary sovereignty means Parliament is the supreme law-making authority. **True**

### 1.3 Prime Minister and Cabinet 首相与内阁

核心句：

> The British Prime Minister is not directly elected by all voters.  
> 英国首相不是由全国选民直接选出的。

必背知识：

- General election 大选选的是 **MPs 国会议员**，不是直接选首相。
- Normally, the party with a majority in the House of Commons forms the government. 通常下议院多数党组阁。
- The leader of that party usually becomes Prime Minister. 该党领袖通常成为首相。
- **Cabinet 内阁**：由重要 ministers 大臣组成，负责主要政策。
- **collective cabinet responsibility 内阁集体负责制**：
  - Cabinet ministers publicly support government decisions. 内阁成员公开支持政府决定。
  - If they cannot support the decision, they should resign. 若不能支持，应辞职。

判断题易错：

- British voters directly elect the Prime Minister. **False**
- The majority party in the House of Commons normally forms the government. **True**
- Cabinet ministers may openly oppose government decisions and stay in Cabinet. **False**

### 1.4 British Constitution 英国宪法

核心句：

> Britain has an uncodified constitution, not a single written constitutional document.  
> 英国宪法是不成文/非单一成典宪法，而不是一部统一的成文宪法典。

必背知识：

- 英国不是没有宪法，而是没有一部单一的 written Constitution。
- 英国宪法来源包括：
  - statutes 成文法
  - common law 普通法
  - conventions 宪法惯例
  - historical documents 历史文件
- 常见历史文件：Magna Carta, Bill of Rights 1689。

判断题易错：

- Britain has no constitution at all. **False**
- Britain has an uncodified constitution. **True**
- The United States has a written Constitution. **True**

### 1.5 Party Politics 政党政治

必背知识：

- **Conservative Party 保守党**：traditionally centre-right, market-oriented, linked with business and tradition.
- **Labour Party 工党**：traditionally centre-left, linked with working class, trade unions and welfare.
- **official opposition 官方反对党**：下议院最大反对党。
- **Shadow Cabinet 影子内阁**：监督对应政府大臣。

判断题易错：

- The official opposition is usually the largest party not in government. **True**
- The Shadow Cabinet belongs to the government. **False**

## 2. 美国政府 American Government

### 2.1 US Constitution 美国宪法

核心句：

> The US Constitution created a federal system with a strong but limited central government.  
> 美国宪法建立了一个强大但受限制的联邦政府。

必背知识：

- Before the Constitution: **Articles of Confederation 邦联条例** created a weak national government.
- Problems under Articles of Confederation:
  - Congress could not effectively collect taxes. 国会无法有效征税。
  - Congress could not force states to obey. 国会无法强迫州服从。
  - states competed with each other. 各州互相竞争。
- **Constitutional Convention 制宪会议**：
  - held in Philadelphia in 1787.
  - created a new Constitution.
- Main principles 主要原则：
  - **federalism 联邦制**
  - **separation of powers 三权分立**
  - **checks and balances 制衡**
  - **limited government 有限政府**
  - **written constitution 成文宪法**

判断题易错：

- The Articles of Confederation created a strong national government. **False**
- The Constitutional Convention was held in Philadelphia in 1787. **True**
- The US Constitution is a written Constitution. **True**

### 2.2 Federalism 联邦制

核心句：

> Federalism divides power between the national government and state governments.  
> 联邦制把权力分配给联邦政府和州政府。

必背知识：

- National/federal government 联邦政府负责 national issues 全国性事务。
- State governments 州政府保留许多本州权力。
- **10th Amendment 第十修正案**：未授予联邦政府、也未禁止州行使的权力，保留给各州或人民。

判断题易错：

- Federalism means all power belongs to the federal government. **False**
- Federalism divides power between national and state governments. **True**

### 2.3 Separation of Powers 三权分立

核心句：

> The US government has three branches: legislative, executive and judicial.  
> 美国政府分为立法、行政、司法三大部门。

| Branch | English | 中文 | Main Power |
|---|---|---|---|
| Legislative | Congress | 国会 | makes laws 立法 |
| Executive | President | 总统 | enforces laws 执法 |
| Judicial | Supreme Court and federal courts | 最高法院和联邦法院 | interprets laws 解释法律 |

必背知识：

- **Congress 国会** = Senate 参议院 + House of Representatives 众议院。
- Senate 参议院：
  - 100 senators, 2 from each state. 100 名参议员，每州 2 名。
  - confirms many presidential appointments. 确认许多总统任命。
  - gives advice and consent on treaties. 对条约提出建议并同意。
- House of Representatives 众议院：
  - 435 voting members. 435 名有投票权议员。
  - seats based on population. 席位按人口分配。
  - starts impeachment. 发起弹劾。
- President 总统：
  - executive branch 行政部门。
  - commander in chief 三军统帅。
  - can veto bills 可否决法案。
  - appoints judges and officials, subject to Senate confirmation. 任命法官和官员，须参议院确认。
- Supreme Court 最高法院：
  - heads the judicial branch. 司法部门最高机构。
  - exercises judicial review. 行使司法审查权。

判断题易错：

- Congress consists of the Senate and the House of Representatives. **True**
- The President belongs to the legislative branch. **False**
- The Supreme Court makes laws. **False**
- The Supreme Court interprets the Constitution. **True**

### 2.4 Checks and Balances 制衡

核心句：

> Checks and balances prevent any one branch from becoming too powerful.  
> 制衡机制防止任何一个部门权力过大。

典型例子：

- President vetoes bills. 总统可以否决国会法案。
- Congress can override a presidential veto by a two-thirds vote. 国会可用三分之二多数推翻总统否决。
- Senate confirms appointments. 参议院确认总统任命。
- Supreme Court can declare laws unconstitutional. 最高法院可宣布法律违宪。
- House impeaches; Senate tries impeachment cases. 众议院弹劾，参议院审判弹劾案。

判断题易错：

- Checks and balances means the President controls all branches. **False**
- The Supreme Court can declare laws unconstitutional. **True**
- The House can impeach federal officials, and the Senate tries impeachment cases. **True**

### 2.5 Bill of Rights 权利法案

核心句：

> The Bill of Rights is the first ten amendments to the US Constitution.  
> 权利法案是美国宪法前十条修正案。

必背知识：

- Added in 1791. 1791 年加入。
- Protects individual liberties from government interference. 保护个人自由免受政府侵犯。
- Important rights:
  - freedom of religion 宗教自由
  - freedom of speech 言论自由
  - freedom of the press 新闻出版自由
  - freedom of assembly 集会自由
  - protection against unreasonable searches 反对无理搜查
  - due process 正当程序
  - jury trial 陪审团审判
  - protection against cruel and unusual punishment 禁止残酷和异常惩罚

判断题易错：

- The Bill of Rights means all constitutional amendments. **False**
- The Bill of Rights was added in 1791. **True**

### 2.6 Presidential Election 总统选举

核心句：

> The US President is chosen through the Electoral College, not by direct national popular vote.  
> 美国总统通过选举人团产生，不是全国普选票直接决定。

必背知识：

- Nomination stage 提名阶段：primary elections 初选 and caucuses 党团会议。
- General election 总统大选：held in November.
- Electoral College 选举人团：
  - total 538 electoral votes.
  - 270 needed to win.
  - candidate can win Electoral College but lose popular vote.

判断题易错：

- The US President is directly elected by simple popular vote. **False**
- The Electoral College has 538 electoral votes. **True**
- A candidate needs at least 270 electoral votes to win. **True**

### 2.7 Two-Party System 两党制

必背知识：

- **Democratic Party 民主党**：
  - modern party usually dated to 1828.
  - symbol: donkey.
  - generally more liberal in modern politics.
  - linked with New Deal, welfare, regulation, social programs.
- **Republican Party 共和党**：
  - formed in the 1850s.
  - symbol: elephant.
  - originally opposed the expansion of slavery into new territories.
  - generally more conservative in modern politics.
- Why two parties dominate:
  - winner-take-all elections 赢者通吃选制。
  - Electoral College 选举人团。
  - ballot access 选票准入困难。
  - voters think third parties cannot win.

判断题易错：

- The Republican Party was originally connected with opposition to the expansion of slavery. **True**
- Third parties usually dominate American presidential politics. **False**

| Topic | UK 英国 | US 美国 |
|---|---|---|
| Constitution 宪法 | uncodified constitution 非单一成典宪法 | written Constitution 成文宪法 |
| Head of State 国家元首 | monarch 君主 | President 总统 |
| Head of Government 政府首脑 | Prime Minister 首相 | President 总统 |

最易考判断：

- The UK has fusion of executive and legislature. **True**
- The US has separation of powers. **True**

## 4. 英国教育 British Education

### 4.1 General Features 总体特点

核心句：

> British education provides literacy and socialisation, but it also reflects class differences.  
> 英国教育提供读写能力和社会化，同时也反映阶级差异。

必背知识：

- Purpose 教育目的：
  - literacy and numeracy 读写与计算能力。
  - socialisation 社会化。
  - prepare citizens and workers 培养公民和劳动者。
- Class issue 阶级问题：
  - education is closely connected with social class.
  - accent, school background and networks may influence careers.
- **old school tie 老校友关系网**：
  - elite school networks that help careers and social status.
- **public schools 公学**：
  - In Britain, public schools are private, fee-paying independent schools.
  - Examples: Eton, Harrow.

判断题易错：

- British public schools are free state schools. **False**
- Eton and Harrow are private, fee-paying independent schools. **True**

### 4.2 1944 Education Act 1944 教育法

核心句：

> The 1944 Education Act expanded free secondary education and created the tripartite system.  
> 1944 年教育法扩大免费中等教育，并形成三轨制。

必背知识：

- free secondary education 免费中等教育。
- tripartite system 三轨制：
  - grammar schools 文法学校。
  - secondary modern schools 现代中学。
  - technical schools 技术中学。
- 11-plus exam 十一岁考试：
  - selected pupils at around age 11.
  - decided which type of secondary school pupils entered.
- Grammar schools 文法学校：
  - selected academically able pupils.
  - important route to university.

判断题易错：

- The 1944 Education Act introduced free secondary education. **True**
- The 11-plus exam was used to select pupils at about age 16. **False**
- Grammar schools selected academically able pupils. **True**

### 4.3 Comprehensive Schools 综合中学

核心句：

> Comprehensive schools admit pupils without selection by academic ability.  
> 综合中学不按学术能力选拔学生。

必背知识：

- introduced widely in the late 1960s.
- aimed to reduce inequality.
- tried to replace the tripartite division.

判断题易错：

- Comprehensive schools select students by the 11-plus exam. **False**
- Comprehensive schools were intended to reduce educational inequality. **True**

### 4.4 National Curriculum and Exams 国家课程与考试

必背知识：

- **National Curriculum 国家课程**：
  - introduced under Conservative reforms.
  - set national standards and core subjects.
- Main stages 主要阶段：
  - early years 幼儿阶段
  - primary education 小学
  - secondary education 中学
  - further education 继续教育
  - higher education 高等教育
- Exams 考试：
  - **GCSE**: usually taken at about age 16.
  - **A-levels**: usually after sixth form, important for university entrance.
  - vocational alternatives 职业教育路径 also exist.

判断题易错：

- GCSE is usually taken at about age 16. **True**
- A-levels are usually connected with university entrance. **True**

### 4.5 British Higher Education 英国高等教育

必背知识：

- **Oxford and Cambridge 牛津剑桥**：
  - oldest and most prestigious universities.
  - often called **Oxbridge**.
- **redbrick universities 红砖大学**：
  - grew in industrial cities.
- **new universities 新大学**：
  - expanded in the 1960s.
- **1992 reform 1992 改革**：
  - many polytechnics became universities.
- **Open University 开放大学**：
  - established in 1969.
  - distance learning 远程教育。
  - important for mature students and lifelong learning.
- Tuition fees 学费：
  - from 1998, tuition fees began for many full-time undergraduates, especially in England and Wales.

判断题易错：

- Oxford and Cambridge are called Oxbridge. **True**
- The Open University is important for distance and adult education. **True**
- Polytechnics became universities in 1992. **True**

## 5. 美国教育 American Education

### 5.1 General Features 总体特点

核心句：

> American education is mainly a state and local matter.  
> 美国教育主要由州和地方管理。

必背知识：

- The US has no single national federal school system. 美国没有单一的全国联邦学校体系。
- Each state has its own education laws. 各州有自己的教育法律。
- Local boards of education 地方教育委员会 help decide:
  - spending 经费。
  - curriculum 课程。
  - teacher standards 教师标准。
  - assessment 评估。
- This reflects the 10th Amendment idea. 这体现第十修正案的州权思想。

判断题易错：

- American education is controlled by one national curriculum. **False**
- American education is mainly controlled by state and local governments. **True**

### 5.2 Public and Private Schools 公立与私立学校

必背知识：

- **public schools 公立学校**：
  - supported by taxpayers.
  - most students attend public schools.
- **private schools 私立学校**：
  - often funded by tuition.
  - many are religious schools.
- Compulsory education 义务教育：
  - all states require school attendance.
  - age limits vary by state, often to 16 or 18.

判断题易错：

- Most American students attend public schools. **True**
- All American private schools are run by the federal government. **False**

### 5.3 Levels and Curriculum 学段与课程

必背知识：

- Elementary school 小学：
  - often kindergarten through grade 5, 6 or 8, depending on district.
- Secondary school 中学：
  - usually middle school/junior high + high school.
  - high school often grades 9-12.
- High school diploma 高中文凭：
  - received before college.
- Curriculum 课程：
  - no single national curriculum.
  - common subjects include English, math, science, social studies, PE, arts and foreign languages.

判断题易错：

- The US has no single national curriculum. **True**
- High school usually comes before college. **True**

### 5.4 Historical Roots and Reform 历史根源与改革

必背知识：

- Puritans valued literacy because people should read the Bible. 清教徒重视读写能力，因为人人应读圣经。
- Harvard College was founded in 1636 to train ministers. 哈佛学院 1636 年建立，最初用于培养牧师。
- Brown v. Board of Education 1954:
  - ruled racial segregation in public schools unconstitutional.
  - 判定公立学校种族隔离违宪。
- Sputnik 1957:
  - pushed the US to strengthen science and technology education.
  - 促使美国加强科技教育。

判断题易错：

- Harvard College was founded in 1636. **True**
- Brown v. Board of Education supported racial segregation. **False**
- Sputnik pushed the US to strengthen science and technology education. **True**

### 5.5 American Higher Education 美国高等教育

必背知识：

- Four types of institutions 四类高等教育机构：
  - **university 大学**：undergraduate colleges + graduate/professional schools.
  - **four-year college 四年制学院**：mainly undergraduate education.
  - **technical training institution 技术培训学校**：practical/professional training.
  - **community college 社区学院**：two-year associate degrees, vocational paths or transfer to four-year colleges.
- Admission factors 录取因素：
  - high school record 高中成绩。
  - teacher recommendations 教师推荐信。
  - interviews 面试，有些学校使用。
  - SAT/ACT scores, where required or submitted.
- Degrees 学位：
  - bachelor's degree 学士学位, often about four years.
  - master's degree 硕士学位。
  - doctoral degree 博士学位。
- GI Bill 退伍军人权利法案：
  - after World War II, helped veterans attend college.
  - changed the image of who could go to college.
- Equal opportunity 教育机会平等：
  - women, African Americans, minorities and non-traditional students gained wider access.
  - affirmative action tried to reduce inequality, but it has been controversial.

判断题易错：

- Community colleges usually offer two-year associate degrees or transfer paths. **True**
- The GI Bill helped many veterans attend college after World War II. **True**
- American higher education is all public and free. **False**

## 7. 美国历史 American History

这是重点。判断题通常考：时间、人物、原因、结果、历史意义。

### 7.1 Before Europeans and Native Americans 欧洲人到来前与印第安人

核心句：

> Native Americans lived in North America before European arrival.  
> 欧洲人到来前，印第安人已经生活在北美。

必背知识：

- Native Americans / American Indians 美洲原住民/印第安人：
  - had diverse cultures.
  - their lives were shaped by land and climate.
- Broader pre-Columbian civilizations 前哥伦布文明：
  - Aztecs 阿兹特克
  - Incas 印加
  - Mayas 玛雅

判断题易错：

- Europeans were the first people to live in North America. **False**
- Native American cultures were diverse. **True**

### 7.2 Jamestown 詹姆斯敦

核心句：

> Jamestown, founded in Virginia in 1607, was the first permanent English settlement in North America.  
> 詹姆斯敦 1607 年建立于弗吉尼亚，是英国人在北美第一个永久殖民地。

必背知识：

- founded in Virginia in 1607. 1607 年建立于弗吉尼亚。
- first permanent English settlement in North America. 英国人在北美第一个永久殖民地。
- tobacco helped the colony survive economically. 烟草帮助殖民地经济存活。
- House of Burgesses 1619:
  - early representative assembly.
  - 早期代议制议会。
- 1619 arrival of Africans:
  - first documented arrival of forcibly captured Africans in English North America.
  - 常被视为英语北美非洲奴隶制历史的重要开端。

判断题易错：

- Jamestown was founded in 1607. **True**
- Jamestown was founded by the French. **False**
- Tobacco helped Jamestown survive economically. **True**
- The House of Burgesses was an early representative assembly. **True**

### 7.3 Puritan New England 清教徒新英格兰

核心句：

> Puritan New England shaped American values such as hard work, education, moral discipline and local self-government.  
> 清教徒新英格兰影响了美国重视勤奋、教育、道德纪律和地方自治的传统。

必背知识：

- Puritans 清教徒：
  - wanted to purify the Church of England.
  - believed in predestination 预定论。
  - valued calling 天职, hard work 勤奋, moral discipline 道德纪律 and Bible reading 读圣经。
- Mayflower 五月花号：
  - sailed in 1620.
- Pilgrims 清教徒移民/朝圣者：
  - signed the Mayflower Compact before landing.
  - settled at Plymouth, Massachusetts.
- Mayflower Compact 五月花号公约：
  - created a civil government for the common good.
  - 体现早期自治精神。
- Massachusetts Bay Colony 马萨诸塞湾殖民地：
  - larger Puritan colony around Boston, 1630.
- Roger Williams 罗杰·威廉斯：
  - argued for separation of church and state.
  - associated with religious freedom in Rhode Island.

判断题易错：

- The Mayflower sailed in 1620. **True**
- The Pilgrims settled at Jamestown. **False**
- The Pilgrims settled at Plymouth, Massachusetts. **True**
- Puritans placed little value on education and Bible reading. **False**
- Roger Williams is associated with religious freedom in Rhode Island. **True**

### 7.4 American Revolution 美国独立战争

核心句：

> The American Revolution began as a conflict over rights and taxation and ended by creating an independent republic.  
> 美国独立战争起源于权利与税收冲突，最终建立了独立共和国。

#### Causes 原因

必背知识：

- After the French and Indian War, Britain wanted the colonies to pay part of imperial costs. 法印战争后，英国要求殖民地承担帝国开支。
- Colonists opposed taxes without representation. 殖民者反对“无代表不纳税”。
- Key slogan:
  - **No taxation without representation. 无代表不纳税。**
- British policies that increased resistance:
  - Stamp Act 印花税法
  - Townshend Acts 唐森德法案
  - Tea Act 茶税法
  - Intolerable Acts 不可容忍法案
- Colonists feared British policies threatened civil liberties. 殖民者担心英国政策威胁公民自由。

判断题易错：

- The American Revolution was mainly caused by the expansion of slavery into western territories. **False**
- "No taxation without representation" was a key slogan. **True**
- The Stamp Act increased colonial resistance. **True**

#### Process 过程

必背时间线：

- 1774: First Continental Congress met in Philadelphia. 第一届大陆会议在费城召开。
- 1775: Fighting began at Lexington and Concord. 莱克星顿和康科德打响战争。
- 1775: Second Continental Congress took charge of the war effort. 第二届大陆会议领导战争。
- July 4, 1776: Declaration of Independence adopted. 独立宣言通过。
- Thomas Jefferson: chief author of the Declaration. 托马斯·杰斐逊是主要起草者。
- France later supported the American side. 法国后来支持美国。
- 1783: Treaty of Paris recognized American independence. 巴黎条约承认美国独立。

判断题易错：

- The Declaration of Independence was adopted on July 4, 1776. **True**
- Thomas Jefferson was the chief author of the Declaration. **True**
- The Treaty of Paris 1783 recognized American independence. **True**

#### Impact 影响

必背知识：

- 13 colonies became the United States of America. 十三个殖民地成为美国。
- Republican government became central. 共和政府成为核心。
- Popular sovereignty 人民主权 became important.
- Declaration expressed:
  - natural rights 自然权利。
  - equality 平等。
  - government by consent 经被统治者同意的政府。
- The Revolution inspired later democratic movements. 激励后来民主运动。
- Contradictions remained:
  - slavery 奴隶制。
  - Native dispossession 原住民土地被夺。
  - women's exclusion 女性被排除在政治权利之外。

判断题易错：

- The Revolution immediately gave equal rights to all women, enslaved people and Native Americans. **False**
- The Revolution helped spread republican and democratic ideals. **True**

### 7.5 From Articles to Constitution 从邦联条例到宪法

核心句：

> The weakness of the Articles of Confederation led to the Constitutional Convention of 1787.  
> 邦联条例的弱点促成了 1787 年制宪会议。

必背知识：

- Articles of Confederation:
  - weak national government.
  - no effective taxation power.
  - difficult to enforce national decisions.
- Constitutional Convention:
  - Philadelphia, 1787.
  - created the US Constitution.
- Constitution:
  - stronger central government.
  - still limited by checks and balances.
  - federal system.

判断题易错：

- The US Constitution was written before the Articles of Confederation. **False**
- The Constitution created a stronger but limited national government. **True**

### 7.6 Civil War 美国内战

核心句：

> The Civil War preserved the Union and destroyed slavery, but it did not end racial conflict.  
> 美国内战维护了联邦并摧毁奴隶制，但没有终结种族冲突。

#### Basic Facts 基本事实

- Time 时间：1861-1865.
- Sides 双方：
  - North / Union 北方/联邦。
  - South / Confederacy 南方/邦联。
- President 总统：Abraham Lincoln 林肯。

判断题易错：

- The Civil War lasted from 1861 to 1865. **True**
- The North was called the Confederacy. **False**
- Abraham Lincoln was president during the Civil War. **True**

#### Causes 原因

必背知识：

- slavery and its expansion into new territories 奴隶制及其是否扩展到新领土。
- conflict between federal power and states' rights 联邦权力与州权冲突。
- economic and social differences:
  - industrial North 工业化北方。
  - plantation South 种植园南方。
- Lincoln's election in 1860 led Southern states to secede. 1860 年林肯当选导致南方州脱离联邦。

判断题易错：

- Slavery and its expansion were central causes of the Civil War. **True**
- The Civil War was mainly fought between Britain and the United States. **False**

#### Key Events 关键事件

必背知识：

- Confederacy formed by Southern states. 南方州组成邦联。
- Emancipation Proclamation 1863:
  - issued by Lincoln.
  - changed the war into a fight against slavery.
- Gettysburg:
  - major turning point.
- South surrendered in 1865.

判断题易错：

- The Emancipation Proclamation was issued in 1863. **True**
- Gettysburg was a major turning point. **True**
- The South won the Civil War. **False**

#### Results 结果

必背知识：

- Union preserved. 联邦得以维护。
- slavery abolished by the 13th Amendment. 第十三修正案废除奴隶制。
- 14th Amendment:
  - citizenship and equal protection.
  - 公民身份和平等保护。
- 15th Amendment:
  - protected Black men's voting rights.
  - 保护黑人男性投票权。
- Reconstruction:
  - tried to rebuild the South.
  - racial inequality continued.

判断题易错：

- The 13th Amendment abolished slavery. **True**
- The 14th Amendment dealt with citizenship and equal protection. **True**
- The 15th Amendment protected Black men's voting rights. **True**
- Reconstruction completely ended racial inequality. **False**

### 7.7 Immigration and American Identity 移民与美国身份

核心句：

> The United States is a nation of immigrants, but immigration has always involved both opportunity and conflict.  
> 美国是移民国家，但移民一直同时带来机会和冲突。

必背知识：

- Native Americans lived there before European arrival. 原住民早于欧洲人生活在北美。
- Africans were brought by force as slaves. 非洲人被强迫带来作为奴隶。
- Major immigration waves:
  - 17th-18th centuries: English and Northern Europeans.
  - 19th century: Germans, Irish, Scandinavians.
  - mid-19th century: Chinese immigrants, Gold Rush and railroads.
  - late 19th and early 20th centuries: Southern and Eastern Europeans.
  - after 1965: more immigration from Asia, Latin America and elsewhere.
- Chinese Exclusion Act 排华法案:
  - restricted Chinese immigration.
- Hart-Celler Act 1965:
  - removed national-origin quotas.
  - increased immigration from Asia and Latin America.
- Melting pot 熔炉:
  - stresses assimilation 同化。
- Salad bowl 沙拉碗:
  - stresses diversity and distinct identities 多元共存。

判断题易错：

- The United States is often described as a nation of immigrants. **True**
- The Chinese Exclusion Act encouraged Chinese immigration. **False**
- The 1965 Hart-Celler Act removed national-origin quotas. **True**
- Melting pot and salad bowl mean exactly the same thing. **False**

  英国是议会民主制和宪政君主制国家。

  议会主权指议会是最高立法机关。
- The British Prime Minister is not directly elected by all voters.  
  英国首相不是由全国选民直接选出。
- The majority party in the House of Commons normally forms the government.  
  下议院多数党通常组阁。
- Britain has an uncodified constitution.  
  英国是不成文/非单一成典宪法。
- The Shadow Cabinet belongs to the opposition.  
  影子内阁属于反对党。

## 2. 美国政府 10 条

- The US has a written Constitution.  
  美国有成文宪法。
- The Articles of Confederation created a weak national government.  
  邦联条例下中央政府弱。
- Federalism divides power between national and state governments.  
  联邦制是联邦政府与州政府分权。
- Three branches: legislative, executive and judicial.  
  三权：立法、行政、司法。
- Congress = Senate + House of Representatives.  
  国会 = 参议院 + 众议院。
- President belongs to the executive branch.  
  总统属于行政部门。
- Supreme Court heads the judicial branch.  
  最高法院是司法部门最高机构。
- Bill of Rights = first ten amendments, added in 1791.  
  权利法案 = 前十条修正案，1791 年加入。
- President is chosen through the Electoral College.  
  总统通过选举人团产生。
- Electoral College: 538 total, 270 needed.  
  选举人团 538 票，270 票获胜。

## 3. 美国历史 15 条

- Native Americans lived in North America before Europeans.  
  原住民早于欧洲人生活在北美。
- Jamestown was founded in Virginia in 1607.  
  詹姆斯敦 1607 年建立于弗吉尼亚。
- Jamestown was the first permanent English settlement in North America.  
  詹姆斯敦是英国人在北美第一个永久殖民地。
- The House of Burgesses in 1619 was an early representative assembly.  
  1619 年市民院是早期代议制机构。
- The Mayflower sailed in 1620.  
  五月花号 1620 年航行。
- The Pilgrims settled at Plymouth, Massachusetts.  
  朝圣者定居普利茅斯。
- Puritans valued hard work, moral discipline and Bible reading.  
  清教徒重勤奋、道德纪律和读圣经。
- No taxation without representation was a key slogan.  
  “无代表不纳税”是关键口号。
- Declaration of Independence: July 4, 1776; chief author Thomas Jefferson.  
  独立宣言 1776 年 7 月 4 日通过，主要作者杰斐逊。
- Treaty of Paris 1783 recognized American independence.  
  1783 巴黎条约承认美国独立。
- Civil War: 1861-1865, Union vs Confederacy.  
  内战 1861-1865，联邦对邦联。
- Slavery and its expansion were central causes of the Civil War.  
  奴隶制及其扩张是内战核心原因。
- Emancipation Proclamation: 1863.  
  解放宣言 1863 年。
- 13th Amendment abolished slavery.  
  第十三修正案废除奴隶制。
- 14th Amendment: citizenship and equal protection; 15th Amendment: Black men's voting rights.  
  第十四修正案涉及公民身份和平等保护；第十五修正案保护黑人男性投票权。

## 4. 英美教育 10 条

- British public schools are private, fee-paying independent schools.  
  英国 public schools 是私立收费学校。
- The 1944 Education Act expanded free secondary education.  
  1944 教育法扩大免费中等教育。
- Tripartite system = grammar + secondary modern + technical schools.  
  三轨制 = 文法学校 + 现代中学 + 技术中学。
- Comprehensive schools admit pupils without academic selection.  
  综合中学不按学术能力选拔。
- GCSE is usually taken at about age 16.  
  GCSE 通常约 16 岁考。
- A-levels are important for university entrance.  
  A-levels 与大学入学有关。
- Oxbridge = Oxford + Cambridge.  
  Oxbridge 指牛津和剑桥。
- American education is mainly state and local.  
  美国教育主要由州和地方管理。
- The US has no single national curriculum.  
  美国没有统一全国课程。
- Community colleges usually offer two-year associate degrees or transfer paths.  
  社区学院通常提供两年副学士学位或转学路径。
